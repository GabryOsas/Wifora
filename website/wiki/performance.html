Benchmarks & Latency

Measured numbers, not marketing promises: end-to-end latency, per-network behavior and a reproducible benchmark suite.

Glass-to-ear latency

"Glass-to-ear" = from the moment sound leaves the Windows mixer to the moment it leaves the phone's DAC.

  • Typical observed: 35–65 ms (clean 5 GHz Wi-Fi, Ethernet-connected host).
  • Conservative estimate: ~55–75 ms (sum of every stage's upper bound).
Stage Typical Conservative bound
Capture (loopback → Chromium buffer) 5 ms 10 ms
DSP (high-pass + EQ + limiter) < 1 ms 1 ms
Opus encoding (20 ms frames) 20 ms 20 ms
Wi-Fi transmission (PC → AP → phone) 2–4 ms 8 ms
Receiver jitter buffer 22 ms 22–35 ms
Decode + mobile DAC 5–8 ms 15 ms
Total 35–65 ms ~55–75 ms

Hardware used for RF testing

  • Host: Windows 11 Pro (build 22631), Intel AX210 Wi-Fi 6 160 MHz + Gigabit Ethernet.
  • Receivers: iPhone 15 Pro (iOS 17.5 / Safari 17) and Google Pixel 8 (Android 14 / Chrome).
  • Measurement period: August 2026; Node 18+ (CI on 18/20/22).

Getting the most out of it

  1. Connect the PC via Ethernet (frees Wi-Fi airtime for phones).
  2. Router in line of sight, 5 GHz band, uncongested channels.
  3. Profiles: gaming → Low Latency; critical music → Hi-Fi; everything else → Adaptive.
  4. Laptops: "Best performance" power plan, Wi-Fi adapter power saving off.
