Elastic Playout & Clock Sync

The secret of crackle-free audio: an AudioWorklet buffer that stretches when needed, shrinks when it can, plus NTP-style clock synchronization.

The three enemies

  • Network jitter: packets arrive with irregular spacing.
  • Packet loss: holes in the stream.
  • Clock drift: the phone's DAC and the PC's differ by tens of ppm: uncorrected, the buffer starves or overflows over time.

ElasticPlayoutBuffer — parameters

Parameter Value Purpose
Stereo ring capacity 240 ms (11,520 × 2 frames) Physical ceiling of the buffer
Target occupancy 80 ms Latency/safety equilibrium point
Startup priming 45 ms Plays only after this initial accumulation
Fade-in after starvation 8 ms Gentle re-entry, never a click
Hold & decay concealment 20 ms In a gap: holds the last sample and fades to zero
Micro-rate correction ±400 ppm, gain 12 ppm/ms Imperceptibly speeds up/slows down to drain or refill
External drift clamp ±150 ppm The clock-sync contribution can't dominate
Catch-up > target + 120 ms If too much accumulated, skip the excess (drop-oldest)
Interpolation linear between samples during rate correction

The AudioWorklet runs at 128-frame quanta (~2.7 ms) and posts telemetry (~every 42 ms) with occupancy, underruns, dropped/late frames. The main thread periodically feeds it the drift correction estimated by clock sync.

Concealment: why no clicks

Instead of repeating samples or hard silence (both audible), the buffer holds the last valid sample with a 20 ms linear decay; when data returns it fades back in over 8 ms. The result is a micro-fade instead of a pop.

NTP-style clock sync

Listener                    Host
   │ ── probe {t0} ──────────▶ │
   │ ◀── reply {t0,t1,t2} ──── │
   t3 = listener local time

RTT     = t3 − t0 − (t2 − t1)
offset  = ((t1 − t0) + (t2 − t3)) / 2
  • Probe every 5 s; rejects: negative RTT, RTT > 500 ms, |offset| > 10 s.
  • Offset and drift (ppm) smoothed with EMA α = 0.2; drift accepted only within ±1000 ppm.
  • Applied correction clamped to ±100 ppm: playbackRate = 1 + ppm/10⁶ (best-effort on the player, always effective on the worklet).
  • The full snapshot goes back to the host (clock.sync report) and shows up in the UI as "Sync ±x.x ms".

Multi-device sync (experimental)

A controller compares the offsets of all listeners in a room (target spread 10 ms, tolerance 40 ms, silent devices ignored after 15 s) and produces ±100 ppm rate advice to converge groups. It's the foundation for future cross-room synchronized sessions.

Why is phone volume quadratic? Human hearing is logarithmic: v² makes mid-slider feel natural. A detail — but you can hear it.
